About

I teach and build in robotics, mobile robotics and industrial automation. I believe students learn best when they get to build, experiment, troubleshoot and solve real problems.

I hold a B.E. in Mechatronics and an M.E. in Industrial Automation and Robotics, and I am completing my PhD in Robotics. My work combines teaching, practical training, project development, research and mentoring.

I am also growing my knowledge in artificial intelligence and research writing, and exploring where AI, robotics and automation meet.

How I mentor students

Mentoring is not giving answers. It is helping students think, explore, build, fail, learn and try again, until the project solves a real problem.

  1. Discuss the ideaUnderstand the problem worth solving.
  2. Choose the technologyFind what is technically possible and pick the right tools.
  3. Build the prototypeHands-on, with troubleshooting as part of learning.
  4. Test and improveRefine into a working product, then take it to hackathons, pitches and exhibitions.
